**Cat Chase, the Android app `com.leocavalcante.catchase`, does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal data.**

There are no analytics, ads, accounts, in-app purchases, or network access. Scores and misses exist only in memory for the current round. The app does not save them on the device or send them to a server.

This page describes the app. The site leocavalcante.dev, where this text is published, uses Google Analytics. That is not part of Cat Chase.

Questions: [lc@leocavalcante.com](mailto:lc@leocavalcante.com).

Effective 21 September 2026.
